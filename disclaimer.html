Independent tool notice

Clear about what Laurel is.

Laurel is an independent student planning tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected to Bicol University.

Unofficial estimates

GWA calculations, honors ranges, eligibility checks, and future-grade scenarios are estimates based on user-entered information. They are not official academic records, certifications, graduation decisions, or a guarantee of an award. Only the appropriate university offices can verify academic standing and final eligibility.

Handbook-based, with an identified edition

Laurel currently models the supplied BU Student Handbook, revised 2019. Rules may differ by applicable curriculum, program, college, graduation cohort, or later university issuance. Confirm the rules that apply to you with your college registrar. Read the verified rules and their sources.

Independent branding

“Bicol University” and “BU” are used descriptively to identify the students and handbook context this tool is intended to support. Laurel uses its own text wordmark and does not use the official university logo or seal. This notice does not assert university permission or approval.

Your input, your control

Profile details and course records are saved in your browser when local storage is available. Laurel's application code does not upload grades, use analytics, request portal credentials, or automatically share results. Static-site hosting may process ordinary request metadata under the host's policies.

Names and degree programs are optional. Downloaded backups contain your records; keep them private. Result summaries exclude your name and program unless you choose to include them. Browser storage does not automatically sync between devices or domains.

How to use a result

Use Laurel to organize your records, explore scenarios, and prepare questions for your adviser or registrar. Check imported data and course exclusions against your official records. Shared results should retain their estimate and non-affiliation labels.
