Reviewed 29 October 2026. This records evidence and unresolved questions, not legal clearance or university approval.

1. The supplied `492555631-BU-Student-Handbook.pdf`, revised 2019. Relevant passages were read locally in full.
2. BU's [official-domain listing of the same handbook](https://bicol-u.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/492555631-BU-Student-Handbook.pdf). Search indexing confirmed the listing; direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. Do not claim the remote PDF was fully read by that research tool.

## Unverified blanket rules

- **NSTP/PE exclusion:** reference tools omit NSTP, but the reviewed BU honors/grading and NSTP-enlistment passages did not supply sufficient basis for automatic exclusion here. Use registrar-directed course inclusion.
- **Permanent DRP ban:** no blanket lifetime disqualification solely for every DRP was established by the reviewed honors text. Flag for registrar review.
- **Underload exceptions:** do not import another university's accepted reasons or fixed minimum-unit threshold. The supplied edition refers to the prescribed curriculum load.
- **Automatic Academic Distinction:** an unmet Latin honors check does not establish the separate requirements for this award.
- **Latest applicability:** the 2019 text and a later revision announcement do not prove that unchanged rules govern every 2026 student.

## University name, activities, and marks

The student-conduct section headed “Unauthorized Solicitation of Contribution/Donation Using the Name of the University” includes wording about activities using the university name, including sponsorship, with prior written approval by the College Dean/OSAS Dean or concerned university officials. It continues across printed pp. 44–45, supplied PDF pp. 54–56.

Preserve that context. It does not establish either unconditional website permission or a universal ban on every descriptive mention. Applicability to planned representation, sponsorship, activities, or official mark use should be resolved with the appropriate university office.

IPOPHL discusses school marks, confusing similarity, and consent in a merchandise context. It does not grant this project clearance. No blanket BU-specific web-logo permission was verified.

Laurel uses its own text wordmark, no official BU logo/seal, a named handbook edition, a non-affiliation notice, and unofficial-result labels. These communicate status; **they do not constitute university authorization, legal clearance, or a guarantee against complaints**. No one has contacted BU or obtained approval on the user's behalf.
