The supplied 2019 handbook is Laurel's calculation reference. Other calculators are workflow references; they are not university authorities.

Primary academic reference

BU Student Handbook · Revised 2019

The supplied copy was checked against its grading, honors, recognition, and student-conduct sections. BU also lists this handbook on its official domain. The online copy returned an access restriction during this review; the supplied local copy was used for the full text. The 2019 edition is not a claim that all current programs follow unchanged rules.

University name, logo, and affiliation

The handbook's student-conduct section on unauthorized solicitation using the university name continues into a requirement for written approval for activities using that name, including sponsorship. See printed pp. 44–45 (PDF pp. 54–56). That context should not be rewritten as either universal permission or a blanket legal ban on every descriptive mention in an independent website.

IPOPHL's guidance on university marks explains that school logos and slogans may be trademarks and discusses confusing similarity and consent. Its example concerns merchandise; it does not grant approval for this website.

Laurel uses an original text wordmark, no BU logo/seal, and a visible non-affiliation notice. A disclaimer is not university authorization. Any planned representation, sponsorship, official mark use, or activity requiring university approval should be resolved with the appropriate university office.

Edition and verification limits

BU has reported a review/revision process for the 2019 handbook. This review did not establish a later complete handbook as the applicable edition for every student. Confirm new issuances and cohort-specific rules with your registrar.
