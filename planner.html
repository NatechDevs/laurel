04 / A practical guide

Use Laurel with confidence

How do I get my grades into the calculator?

Use your student portal, certified grade report, or transcript as the source. Add each course with its units and final grade, or import a CSV. The tool does not log into your BU account or verify records with the university.

For a spreadsheet, use columns Course, Units, Grade, Semester. Semester is optional. Import supports comma-separated CSV, tab-separated text, quoted course names, and an optional header row.

What does the result actually mean?

GWA range is a mathematical comparison with the honors thresholds. Potentially eligible means your complete entered record and self-reported checks meet the listed conditions. It is always an estimate. Missing records, unresolved grades, or unmet requirements are shown explicitly.

What should I do with INC, DRP, PE, or NSTP?

INC, DRP, and PENDING are excluded from the numeric average and flagged for review. A DRP entry does not automatically disqualify you; its effect requires registrar guidance. Include degree credits according to your registrar’s instructions. PE and NSTP are not automatically excluded. An excluded failing grade still appears in the honors assessment.

Can I use my records on another device?

Download a JSON backup, then restore it on your other device. Records stay in this browser’s local storage and do not sync. Clearing browser data may remove them. A CSV export includes all course rows and semester names, but not your profile, eligibility checks, or inclusion settings; use JSON for a complete backup.

How do sharing and privacy work?

The result card includes your GWA, scope, course count, and assessment status. Your name and program are excluded unless you select them. Your course list is never included in the card. Download or copy a summary, then share it yourself. No grades or profile data are uploaded by the application.

Policy notes & calculation method

Reference: BU Student Handbook, revised 2019, printed pp. 18–21 (PDF pp. 29–31). This tool follows the supplied edition; confirm any newer rules with your college registrar.

  • GWA = Σ(grade × units) ÷ Σ(units). All semester records are weighted by units, rather than averaging semester GWAs. Honors comparisons use the GWA rounded to four decimal places.
  • 1.0 is the highest; 3.0 is passing; 5.0 is failure. The handbook lists 3.1–4.0 as conditional. Enter only the final rating on your official record.
  • INC is excluded until resolved. INC, DRP and pending grades are not treated as zero. Unresolved records prevent an eligibility estimate. An overdue INC may become a failing grade; update it according to your registrar’s record.
  • Only credits toward the degree belong in the final average. Use “In GWA” to exclude a course when instructed by your registrar; this tool does not automatically exclude PE or NSTP. Excluded low grades still trigger a record warning.
  • Latin honors require more than the GWA. The handbook requires regular load, curriculum compliance, college residency, and no grade lower than 3.0 in any subject. Underloading disqualifies a student from Latin honors under this edition.
  • Academic distinction has its own conditions. For a candidate meeting the honors GWA but missing other honors requirements, the handbook describes “with Academic Distinction,” subject to no grade lower than 3.0, curriculum and transfer-credit rules, at least 75% of curriculum units earned at BU, and at least six immediately preceding semesters/terms in residence. Ask the registrar to evaluate this separately; the tool does not automatically award it.
