A note on academic honors

A GWA range is not
an honors confirmation.

Laurel references the BU Student Handbook, revised 2019. Latin honors also depend on your full academic history and non-grade requirements. Even a “potentially eligible” result is a self-assessed estimate.

Your college evaluation committee makes the final determination. Confirm the rules that apply to your program and graduation cohort with your registrar.

Before you begin

A few things to know.

Simple answers to the questions that matter before you enter your grades.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start immediately. Your name and degree program are optional, and Laurel does not ask for a student ID or portal password.

Can Laurel confirm my Latin honors?

Laurel calculates a GWA range and checks your entered record against handbook requirements. Even a potentially eligible result is a self-assessed estimate. Your college evaluation committee makes the final decision.

Do I have to enter every course manually?

You can enter courses individually, paste spreadsheet cells, or import a CSV file. Use Course, Units, Grade, and an optional Semester column. A downloadable template is available inside the planner.

Will I lose my grades when I leave the page?

Laurel saves your workspace in this browser when local storage is available. Records do not sync between devices or website addresses. Download a JSON backup before clearing browser data, switching devices, or moving to a different URL.
